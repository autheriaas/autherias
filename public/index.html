How it works

One path from idea to delivery, with no surprises along the way.

  1. Share your idea

    Send reference art and a description through the form below. You'll get a rough sketch and a scope for your tier back.

  2. Reserve your slot

    A 25–50% deposit via PayPal or Ko-fi secures your place in queue. Full rendering starts once it clears.

  3. Sketch & revisions

    You'll see the rough sketch first and can flag changes before final rendering begins — Basic/Medium wrap in 2–3 weeks, Premium is scoped per project.

  4. Approve & deliver

    Review a final preview, pay the remaining balance, then get your high-resolution files in the format you need.

FAQ

Cannot find your answer? Email autheriawork@gmail.com.

What kind of art do you make?+

Original character illustrations, 3D character renders and assets, mascot & logo design, and PFPs, banners & stream emotes — check the Portfolio and "3D & More" sections above for examples.

How much does a commission cost?+

Pricing depends on the scope of the piece. Commissions are grouped into Basic, Medium and Premium tiers by detail and complexity rather than a fixed price list — send your idea through the form below and you'll get a personal quote back.

How long does a commission take to complete?+

Basic and Medium tier pieces typically take 2–3 weeks. Premium tier pieces take longer and are scoped individually depending on complexity.

How much of a deposit do I need to pay upfront?+

A 25–50% deposit reserves your slot in the queue. Sketch work only begins once that deposit has cleared.

When is the remaining balance due?+

Once the final piece is ready, you'll get a preview to review and approve. The remaining balance is only due after you approve it — and it's paid before high-resolution files are delivered.

What payment methods do you accept?+

PayPal and Ko-fi are the main options, chosen for being simple and professional. Other payment methods are available too — just mention it when you reach out.

Can I get a refund if I change my mind?+

Deposits are fully refundable as long as sketch work hasn't started yet. Once work is underway, refunds are handled case by case — email me directly and we'll sort out something fair.

Can I request changes while it's being made?+

Yes — every commission starts with a rough sketch stage, so you can flag changes before final rendering begins.

What do you need from me to start a commission?+

Just fill out the commission form: your name and email, the commission type, an optional budget, a character description, and reference images if you have them. That's enough to reply with a scope and quote.

Can I use the finished piece commercially?+

Personal use is included by default. Commercial or brand use — mascots, logos, merchandise, etc. — can be arranged, just mention your intended use in the commission form.
